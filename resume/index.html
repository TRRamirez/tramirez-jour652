A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Taylor Ramirez

Hello! I'm Taylor, a Master of Journalism student at the University of Maryland's Philip Merrill College of Journalism and a fellow with the Howard Center for Investigative Journalism.

Originally from Montgomery County, Maryland, my interests lie at the intersection of investigative reporting, politics and digital media. I’m particularly interested in translating complex investigative stories into accessible, engaging social media content, helping audiences better understand the issues shaping their world.

I also enjoy exploring how news organizations can build strong brand identities, develop effective strategies and connect with audiences in an evolving digital landscape. My passion for politics drives my interest in finding new ways to make political reporting more digestible for the next generation of news consumers.

Outside of the newsroom and the classroom, you can find me nose-deep in a book, baking, or cuddling with my dogs.
